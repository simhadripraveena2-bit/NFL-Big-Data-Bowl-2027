**Title:** Licensing of aggregate results, Writeups and Notebooks that combine code with findings

Hi hosts, thank you for the competition. I'd like to check how the data and winner-licence rules apply to public Writeups and Notebooks before publishing mine.

**My reading of the rules:**
- Competition-specific rule 7 lists "Data Access and Use: **CC BY-NC 4.0**".
- §2.4.a permits non-commercial use of the Competition Data, including participation and academic research.
- §2.4.b prohibits publishing or redistributing the Competition Data to non-participants.
- §2.5.a.1 requires winners to license "your winning Submission and the source code used to generate the Submission" under an OSI-approved licence that does not limit commercial use.

**My questions:**

1. **Aggregate findings.** May participants publish aggregate statistical findings in a public competition Writeup and a public Kaggle Notebook, provided no raw data or player-level extracts are distributed? By aggregate findings I mean estimates, confidence intervals, sample sizes and figures.

2. **Scope of the winner licence.** Does the OSI/commercial-use requirement in §2.5.a.1 apply to the written report and its aggregate statistical results, or only to the software and model code?

3. **Marking mixed content.** A public notebook can contain Apache-2.0 source code alongside aggregate results and figures derived from the Competition Data. How should participants mark those derived results and figures?

4. **Recommended wording.** Do you recommend a particular attribution or licensing statement for these mixed-content submissions? For example: "Derived from NFL Big Data Bowl 2027 competition data, used under the competition rules."

Thanks!
